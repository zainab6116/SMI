Welcome to SMI

Sir Mazhar Institute is an educational institute in Gulberg Town, Karachi, committed to helping students grow academically and move toward their future goals.

We support learners from the early school years through Intermediate and degree level. For more than a decade, families in our community have trusted us with their education.

Our aim is simple: a learning environment where students can understand their subjects, build confidence and make steady progress.
